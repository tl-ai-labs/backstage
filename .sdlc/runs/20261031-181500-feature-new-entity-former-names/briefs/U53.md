### U53 — edit `docs/features/software-catalog/life-of-an-entity.md` (docs)

Behaviour: Documents renames: behaviour, conflict rules and limits.

Rules:
1. Say what the reader gets first: declare former refs and old entity-page and TechDocs links redirect with a notice, while a rename nobody declares does not redirect.
2. Conflict rules: a claim on a live entity with the same location key waits and takes effect once that entity is gone; a claim on a live entity from another source is rejected and stays rejected until the value is removed, even after that entity disappears, and to clear it you remove the value, then add it back once the old entity is gone.
3. Competing claims: the earliest recorded claim wins and others get an error; the winner is stable across reprocessing and restarts, equal times break on the claimant's ref, and the next-earliest takes over when the winner is removed. Which of two brand-new claims is recorded first depends on processing order.
4. A new entity taking the name: exact lookups always win, the claim is inactive with no error, and it takes effect again if that entity goes away.
5. Claim problems show up as entity processing errors and never stop the entity from being ingested.
6. Limits: one hop only, same kind only, a former ref of an orphan kept by `catalog.orphanStrategy: keep` does not redirect because a kept orphan is live, relations and other stored refs are not rewritten, and published TechDocs content is not moved or copied.
